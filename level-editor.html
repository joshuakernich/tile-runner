Paint walls by dragging. Start / Tile / Head are the 3 pre-laid track cells (Head = init·2, the runner's front).
Slider block: one click drops the block on a 3-cell rail, with a nook each side. Drag the block to slide it along its rail (that's its starting cell); drag it past an end or sideways off the rail and the whole structure moves. Then drag either end handle (the ringed dots) to lengthen, shorten or swing the rail round — pull an end sideways and the rail rotates. Dragging the block itself slides it along the rail; pull it past an end or off the line and the whole structure moves. The rail is where the block slides to; it needs a dead-end nook on the far side.
Saw: one click drops a saw on a 3-cell rail (uses the speed + phase boxes); clicking an existing saw re-applies those boxes to it. Drag either end handle to lengthen, shorten or swing the rail round — it pivots on the opposite end. Drag the blade along the rail to set its starting phase (0 = parked at the low end, 0.5 = at the high end) — stagger a row of saws this way so they don't sweep as one line. Drag the blade past an end or off the line, or drag any other rail cell, to move the whole saw.
Stone: drag to paint breakable stone blocks (the hp box sets how many taps to smash). Right-drag or drag over a stone to remove.
Platform: fixed free track cast into a wall. Click an empty cell to place it in the selected shape; click it again to cycle through the six shapes; right-click to remove. The runner rides it for free but can only enter/leave through the two drawn ends — the reachability check knows this.
Speed: the level’s starting pace in tiles/sec (cps). Every shipped level starts at 0.80.
Talisman: a one-off collectible. Click to place the one chosen in the dropdown, click again to cycle, right-click to remove. It does not block track — put it somewhere the runner can flow through, never at the end of a spur, or it can't be collected.
Key / Door: pick a colour swatch first — a key opens the door of the same colour.
Autosave keeps every edit in your browser, so you can close/refresh and pick up where you left off (levels you’ve changed show a * in the Load list). It does not touch the game — when a batch is ready, tell me “take the autosave” and hit Copy full levels.js (or Download) so I can convert it into the real game data. “Reset to shipped” wipes the draft back to the current game levels.
