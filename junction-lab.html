Junction Lab

The leading tile's 3-way junction: the three routes out of it, stacked rather than unioned. Each route is a complete little tile of its own — ground, worn skin, beam and the white lip along its own top edge — rendered next door, cut to its own shape, and only then laid down at its weight. They go down faintest first: right, straight, left, so the route the tile is most sure of is the one on top. Each is cut with destination-in rather than a globalAlpha, because the ground carries an alpha of its own that a per-layer alpha would overwrite. The rail is hidden, as it is in the game. Geometry and drawing are lifted from index.html. Tune, then Copy JUNCTION block.

The three routes

Each route is a finished layer laid over the last, right then straight then left, so where they overlap the strongest one WINS rather than the alphas adding up — a route at 1.00 is solid wherever it runs and nothing under it shows through. Left and right are mirror images, so setting them apart tilts the tile.
