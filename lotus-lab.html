Drag the joints. He is drawn by the GAME — __TR.lotusJoints() paints the shipped figure into this page's canvas and hands back where every joint of it actually landed, so the handles sit on the pose rather than on a diagram of it, and what you are shaping is what ships. Dragging writes straight into POSES.lotus in the running game. Serve this page (python3 tools/serve.py 8123); off the filesystem the frame cannot be read.

The joints

KNEE sets how far out and how far down. FOOT sets how far back in it tucks, and how far down — and the two feet move independently in height (that is cross; level is nought). TOE is the little bone off the end: its length and the angle it points.

near leg — drawn in front far leg — behind

One thing to know: a pose is written as a few numbers, not as four free points. How far out the knees sit and how far in the feet tuck are ONE number each, shared by both legs, so dragging either knee moves both. Only the two feet's heights are separate.

The rest of it

Not positions, so not handles. Sits lower by drops the whole figure toward the track, in cells — a folded leg stops well above the line a standing foot is on, so without it he hangs over the ground with a gap nothing in the pose can close. Then the slow swell the fold rides on.
