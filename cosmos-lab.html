clustering is the one to play with. At 0 the field is placed by one hash per star — evenly scattered, which is the one thing a real sky never is. At 1 every star is pulled into a drift, leaving voids between them. In between the two positions are blended, so no star is ever in two places. …per drift is how many share a middle and …spread how far from it they may fall, in patches.
