The map

A face in perspective is not a parallelogram, and flat — one affine frame for the whole side, which is what this used to do — cannot draw anything but one. The mark sheared off the face it was supposed to be cut into. Warped lays it on through the face's own lattice, a cell at a time.

The standing solid shows its OUTSIDE, and the outside is the charms: each side is the material its badge is made of, with the badge cut into it. As a side falls it turns over and the INSIDE comes up — a chapter's own quilt, with nothing written on it. turns at is how far through that fall the change happens and over is how much of the fall it takes. grain is how far the nine squares of a charm's side step either side of its colour; at zero the side is one flat swatch.
