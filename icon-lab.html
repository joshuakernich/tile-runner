Take it away

Saves icon-1024, icon-512, icon-192 and icon-180, named for the manifest. Drop them in beside index.html and bump CACHE in sw.js.

Nothing here is redrawn. The lab hands the running game a canvas and a size and the game paints the icon into it with the routines the board itself uses — drawBackground for the void, drawGlass and drawPlatformSurface for the tiles, paintRunner for him. There is no second copy of the art to drift, which is what the old version of this page was.

The dashed circle on the 512 is roughly what Android keeps of a maskable icon. Anything outside it may be cropped away on some launchers. This page must be SERVED — file:// stops it reading the frame.
