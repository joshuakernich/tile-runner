1 · The wide black

The place's shade on the sky round it. Width is in tiles; strength is what it reaches right at the edge.

2 · The colour

The colour of the tile or patch nearest each stretch of edge, made vivid. core is how wide its bright edge is (a share of width); tail is how strong the rest of it is out to the full width (a share of strength). A small core and a low tail is a colour that drops away fast.

3 · The edge

A thin black hugging the boundary, so the colour starts a hair off it.
