Their box

0.80

A fraction of the body each monster is DRAWN at, not a fixed size — so a rail monster (0.62 of a cell) gets a smaller box than a biter (0.86) off one number. 0.78 is its inscribed panel exactly: 1 − 2 × MONSTER.panelPad. 1.00 is the whole tile, teeth excluded.

What is touching

Distance between the two boxes at their closest, in cells, measured the way the game measures it. Negative means they are overlapping — which is a death, unless the thing is a trap holding itself open.
