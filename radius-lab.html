Radius

0.0

A fraction of one cell. 0.5 is a circle; 0 is a hard square. Shipped is 0.

Board

5

Columns across the specimen board. Fewer columns = bigger tiles. A radius that reads well on a small board can look like a blob on a big one, so check both.

Export

Reload

Picks up edits to index.html without losing your slider position.

Every tile-shaped thing in the game takes its corners from one number — tileR(s) = s * TILE.radius — so this single slider moves all of them at once. On the board above, top to bottom: free tiles you can drag (with their grip dots), the laid track including a corner and the leading junction, walls, a fixed platform, stone at three strengths, a door and its key, a rift, a coin, a talisman, a slider block on its rail, and monsters — a hunter, a stealth, a trap and a rail. The runner's own body has its own corner and does not follow this slider.
