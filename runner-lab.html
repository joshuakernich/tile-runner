This is the same drawing code as the game, reading the same RUNNER block that lives in index.html just above drawRunner. Tweak, then Copy RUNNER block and paste the whole thing over the one in index.html — replace it wholesale rather than hand-patching values.
Lengths are fractions of a cell, so the runner scales with the board; the strip on the right of the canvas shows him at real in-game sizes. Onion skin ghosts the stride either side of the current pose, Guides marks the track surface, hip line, body box and knee points, and the stride slider scrubs one full step cycle when paused.
Knees bends each leg with two-bone IK, so the bend stays correct as the foot swings rather than being faked per frame. bend amount is slack relative to the hip-to-foot distance, so the bend stays even across the whole stride instead of folding up at mid-step; thigh share slides the knee up or down the leg; forward lead exaggerates the bend past what the IK gives you. As of v5.6 the game draws these too, so what you see here is what ships.
