The cube opening

The moment the Tiloid is thrown open — the last of its turn, the flash it opens inside, the six charms and him going up out of it, and the camera's rush in. It REPLAYS ON A LOOP in the frame beside you, for as long as you like: the lab holds the film's clock itself and steps it, so any window — two seconds round the pop, or the WHOLE INTRO end to end — comes round again and again at whatever speed you want, and every slider lands on the next pass. It does not stop at the end of the film and it does not have to be restarted: a seek takes down anything standing over the film and puts the film back up if it has gone. Nothing is drawn here — that is the shipped film, in an iframe, pushed through __TR.openTune() and re-seeked, so what you are tuning cannot drift from what ships. Serve this page (python3 tools/serve.py 8123); off the filesystem the frame cannot be read.

The turn into it

The tumble the shockwave starts. It does not come to a halt — it is still gathering speed on the frame the cube bursts, and the flash covers the instant the turning stops.

The opening

When the cube opens, how long it takes to lie flat, and how long the map then takes to unfold out of it. The launch and the map's start are chained to these — they are one event.

The flash it opens inside

The bloom that hides the moment itself — white at the core, the rainbow past it.

What it throws

Him and the six charms, each on its own long arc out of the box.

The camera

Three movements, and then the stepped pull-back. It is THROWN BACK off the blast, HELD there while the charms and he come up out of it, and only then goes all the way in and finds him. 1 is the framing that holds the whole scene and bigger is closer — so the throw-back stops short of 1 on purpose: at 1 everything that just came out of the cube is a speck.

The cube itself

Its edges taper from the corner nearest you to the one furthest; its sides are rainbow glass with a wide curved sheen crossing them.
