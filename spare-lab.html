Take it away

The board above carries two spare tiles — the two out on their own, with the wheel turning in the grip — and one ordinary free tile for comparison, which appears behind the runner once he has passed one. A spare is a tile the level left lying out there, or one the Mushroom dropped: it is not yours until the track reaches it, so it cannot be dragged, cannot be called to a junction and does not count toward the tiles you are holding.

Nothing here is redrawn. The lab pushes the SPARE block straight into the running game through __TR.spare(), so what you are looking at is the shipped renderer and not a copy of it that could drift. This page must be SERVED — file:// stops it reading the frame.
