The hue runs round the circle rather than along it, so the tail behind the hand carries a drift of colour with it instead of the whole ring being one shade at a time. Keep tint low — the Bell's tones are near-black, so a little colour goes a long way over them.

The tones

The Bell's two tones are near-black — what it does is subtract something from the world, so it is drawn in the colour of that. Alternating them square by square gives the circle its grain; one tone makes it a solid shadow.

Try it

Check what it unmakes builds a board with monsters standing at one, two and three tiles, rings once, and says which of them are still there.

Take it away

The runner crawls up the middle of a field of stone so he stays put, and rings on a loop. The Bell takes monsters, not rock, so the stone is only there to give the circle something to fall across — it stands all day, which is what makes it a good backdrop for tuning.
